📖 About This Website

Learn what this website is for and how to use it.

What is this website?

This is a community website made for the residents of Barangay Molino 3, Bacoor City, Cavite. It gives everyone a simple way to report lost items, raise community concerns, and suggest improvements for the barangay — all in one place.

Anyone can post and anyone can read. No account needed. Just open the website, fill in the details, and submit.

What can you do here?

Lost & Found

Did you lose something? Post it here with a description and your contact number so others can help you find it. Did you find something? Post it too so the owner can contact you.

Complaints

See a problem in the barangay — like a broken road, flooding, or illegal dumping? Post it here.

Recommendations

Have an idea to improve the barangay? Share it here.

How to post a Lost or Found item

  1. Go to the Lost & Found page.
  2. Click the Post Item tab.
  3. Choose whether you lost something or found something.
  4. Fill in the item name, description, where it was lost or found, and your contact number.
  5. You can add a photo if you have one — it helps a lot.
  6. Click Post Item to submit.

Once posted, your item will appear in the Lost or Found tab for everyone to see. When the item is returned, click Mark Resolved to move it to the Resolved tab.

How to post a Complaint

  1. Go to the Complaints page.
  2. Click the Post Complaint tab.
  3. Write a short title and a description of the problem.
  4. Add a photo if possible — it makes the complaint clearer.
  5. Click Submit Complaint.

Once posted, your item will appear in the active tab for everyone to see. When the complaint is resolved, An admin from the barangay will mark it as Resolved to move it to the Resolved tab.

How to post a Recommendation

  1. Go to the Recommendations page.
  2. Click the Post Recommendation tab.
  3. Write a title and explain your idea.
  4. Add a photo if needed — it makes the recommendation clearer.
  5. Click Submit Recommendation.

Once posted, your item will appear in the active tab for everyone to see. When the complaint is resolved, An admin from the barangay will mark it as Resolved to move it to the Resolved tab.

A few reminders

  • Be honest and respectful in your posts.
  • Do not post personal information beyond what is needed.
  • Photos should be clear and relevant to your post.
  • Anyone can see what you post — this is a public website.
  • Posts can be marked as resolved once the issue is addressed.
